The 'Bad Apple' ASCII art is derived from https://github.com/Chion82/ASCII_bad_apple
which in turn comes from the 'Nico Nico Douga' story-boarded music video that was
rendered by a group led by 'Anria'. The video is for the Alstroemeria Records'
version of Bad Apple!! from the soundtrack of the Touhou Project game Lotus Land Story.
