## N-1 of These People Are Lying

This is a Discord implementation of the Technical Difficulties game "Two of these people are lying".
To be able to play, you need to submit a non-stub English language wikipedia article, which you can remember some details from.
A number of players then form a game, and one of their articles are chosen.
Each player much convince the game leader that they submitted the article by explaining what it's about, coming up with a fabrication on the fly.

You score a point for tricking people into thinking your made up explanation is what's in the article!

Sub-commands:
- `!lie add https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vosper%20Thornycroft` will add an article to your submitted articles
- `!lie remove https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vosper%20Thornycroft` will remove an article from your submitted articles
- `!lie list` will show your currently submitted articles
- `!lie play` will start a new game -- bluffers will need to sign up for each game!

Note that the parentheticals in the link will be automatically removed, so disambiguation hints will not be shown as part of the game.
However, I recommend not picking something with a lot of articles, because it will be more likely that someone can fake a different one of them.
